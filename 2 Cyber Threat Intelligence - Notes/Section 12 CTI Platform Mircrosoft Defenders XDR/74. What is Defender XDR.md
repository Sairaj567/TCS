## Detailed Notes

### Lecture Flow

We discussed XDR earlier and we defined it accordingly.
But what is Microsoft Defender XDR?
This one is a unified extended detection and response service, which includes a lot of other services from Microsoft.
But defender XDR is actually one portal that your security operations center can access and do [[1 Cybersecurity Foundation - Notes/1 – Introduction to Cybersecurity|incident response]] in there.

### And the services that are included in the portal are, for example, defen

And the services that are included in the portal are, for example, defender for endpoint, which focuses on protecting endpoints and servers from threats.
But we also have defender for identity focused on domain controllers.
We have defender for office strictly focused on collaboration tools such as teams outlook also then exchange online but also word documents.
everything that's really part of the Microsoft Office suite.

### Then we have defender for cloud apps

Then we have defender for [[2 Cyber Threat Intelligence - Notes/Section 3 AZURE/11. Cloud Computing Properties|cloud]] apps.
acting as a cloud access security broker.
this one shall protect us from threats that, are focused on, software as a service, applications and the interaction of users with those.
Then we have defender for vulnerability management focused on, identifying vulnerabilities and prioritizing them, categorizing them accordingly.

### Then we have entry ID protection

Then we have entry ID protection.
This one is totally focused on protecting identities that reside in [[2 Cyber Threat Intelligence - Notes/Section 3 AZURE/17. Entra ID Tenants and Azure Subscriptions|entra]].
And we also have data loss prevention.
All of this is part of the defender XDR portal.
and this is what it is about.
