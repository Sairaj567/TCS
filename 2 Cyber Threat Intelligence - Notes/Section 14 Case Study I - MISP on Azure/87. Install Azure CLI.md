## Detailed Notes

### Lecture Flow

What we will do as a first step after creating the [[2 Cyber Threat Intelligence - Notes/Section 3 AZURE/16. Azure Subscription Types|subscription]] is to create a resource group.
And for that, just head over for Portal.azure.com.
And then we click on either resource groups or we look for it in the search and click on Resource Groups.
From there we click on create.

### And what we will do then is we will select our subscription

And what we will do then is we will select our subscription.
In my case it will be Azure Security demos.
But in your case it will be a different name.
And the name of the resource group for this case study will be Misp.

### The region can be completely of your choice

The region can be completely of your choice.
Doesn't matter at all.
In my case it will be East US.
We can ignore the next tags button and instead immediately click on Review and Create.

### And then again click on recreate after the validation has passed

And then again click on recreate after the validation has passed.
And after a few seconds you already see that the resource group has been created.
And that's actually it for this section already.
