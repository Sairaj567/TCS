### Lecture Flow

You just learned about all the roles that exist and that you can leverage as part of Azure [[1 Cybersecurity Foundation - Notes/2 – Core Concepts in Cybersecurity|Rbac]], especially for Sentinel.
What we will do now is we will have a closer look at how to assign such roles in Azure.
what we will do for this is we will go on to resource groups and select the resource group where your Sentinel [[2 Cyber Threat Intelligence - Notes/Section 16 Case Study III - Leveraging CTI in Microsoft Sentinel/109. Demo - Create a Log Analytics Workspace|workspace]] resides.

we select it.

### And then we go on to the access control part and click on Role Assignmen

And then we go on to the access control part and click on Role Assignments.
And under Role Assignments, what you can actually see is, first of all, the role assignments that are existing in the [[2 Cyber Threat Intelligence - Notes/Section 3 AZURE/16. Azure Subscription Types|subscription]].
And as you can see, there is a limit of 4000 role assignments per subscription.
This is something that you can actually also increase by raising a support request if you run into those limits.
But what you also see is that you currently have zero role assignments.
But because we want to add a new role assignment, what we do is we click on add.

### And click on Add a New Role assignment

And click on Add a New Role assignment.
And what you can then do is we can look for Sentinel.
And as you can see.
All the central roles that we discussed earlier are arriving.
what we do is we click on Central Contributor.
Click on next.

### And then under the members plate, what we can actually do is we can deci

And then under the members plate, what we can actually do is we can decide whether we want to assign this access to a user, group or service principle that is the first option, or to go with the managed identity instead.
Managed identity is something that you can and should heavily utilize when you build, for example, automations in Sentinel or when to when you want to assign permissions and especially roles therefore to Azure resources.

Click on select members.
