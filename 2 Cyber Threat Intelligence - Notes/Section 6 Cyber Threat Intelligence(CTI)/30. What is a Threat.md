## Detailed Notes

### Lecture Flow

let's start with our basics for cyber threat intelligence.
And the first question we need to ask ourselves in this context is what is a threat?
And to answer this question we will leverage the definition from NIST.
And this one says a threat is any circumstance or event with the potential to adversely impact organizational operations, organizational assets, or individuals through an information system.
This one is important through an information system via unauthorized access, destruction, disclosure, modification of information, and or denial of service.

### And why did I put an emphasis on through information system

And why did I put an emphasis on through information system?
The purpose is that we talk about threats in the context of cybersecurity, because for an enterprise, there are a lot of other threats out there that have nothing to do with cybersecurity.
Threats like this are, for example, natural disasters, financial threats such as inflation.
And there are a lot of other threats that are not related to cybersecurity at all.
But because we talk about cyber threat intelligence, we talk about cyber threats.

### And what you see on the screen is the definition for a cyber security th

And what you see on the screen is the definition for a cyber security threat.
That is important to consider because for an enterprise there is way, way more than just cybersecurity in the context of cyber threat intelligence.
We don't talk about inflation, we don't talk about natural disasters, but we talk about threats, that are carried out through an information system.
