## Detailed Notes

### Lecture Flow

The next important term in attack is the data sources.
And what data sources do is they provide the actual source that you need in terms of collecting telemetry and the data sources.
The purpose of them is to help you identify the correct data source, to actually combat the ttps that the adversaries are leveraging.
if we look at the combination of tactic, technique and sub technique that we discussed earlier, which is reconnaissance as the tactic, active scanning as the technique and vulnerability scanning as the sub technique, the attack framework would tell you that a data source that you could onboard into your security system, such as your CIM system, would be network traffic.

### And this is quite generic and a very high level, but it still points you

And this is quite generic and a very high level, but it still points you into the correct direction.
And if we drill this a bit more down and go further into detail, what could this mean?
Well, if we talk about vulnerability scanning this could be things like, the logs of your web application firewall.
This could be logs of your on premises network, anything that allows you to detect vulnerability scanning on a network level.
That is the entire purpose of a data source in the middle attack framework.
