## Detailed Notes

### Lecture Flow

The last term that we will define as part of Mitratech is the so-called campaigns.
what is a campaign?
A campaign is an intrusion activity that is conducted over a specific period of time, with common targets and objectives.
And the most important takeaway for you here is not every successful breach against an enterprise is a campaign.
If an enterprise gets owned, even with a big ransomware [[2 Cyber Threat Intelligence - Notes/Section 7 CTI Related Frameworks/Diamond model, LM Cyber Kill Chain, MITRE ATTACK|attack]] or something, which could result in millions and millions of dollars of loss, this is not necessarily a campaign because it doesn't fulfill the criteria.
It's really about a specific period with common targets and objectives.

### whenever you have

whenever you have.
A single enterprise that is being compromised.
That is rarely a campaign.
It can be, but it is rarely a campaign.
Campaigns are really the big.
Organized operations that are carried out in most cases across multiple targets, and in most cases they are conducted by either a nation state actor or a very well organized criminal group.

### And what you see here is two examples of those

And what you see here is two examples of those.
The first one being the campaign with the ID 25 and the end.
The name for this one is the attack on the Ukraine electric power grid in 2016.
And there is a [[2 Cyber Threat Intelligence - Notes/Section 6 Cyber Threat Intelligence(CTI)/29. Intelligence, Threat Intelligence and Cyber Threat Intelligence|threat]] actor involved that is called sandworm.

### They also consist of several threat actors

They also consist of several threat actors.
But sandworm is a really, really, highly known name across the security industry.
And what they did is they leveraged several ttps to disrupt the Ukrainian power grid.
And this campaign was actually the second major attack against Ukraine by the sandworm team.

### They already did that in 2015

They already did that in 2015.
And so this is a really popular campaign, popular in a negative way, but really popular in the sense of it gained a lot of attention in the security community also because critical infrastructure was involved, which is the Ukrainian power grid.
And then secondly, we have an operation called cuckoo bees.
This one was also very famously in the news, because this one was a cyber espionage campaign that targeted a lot of manufacturing companies in East Asia, Western Europe, North America since at least 2019.

And if we look at the campaign more closely, what was also identified was they the main target was to steal proprietary information, research, development documents, source code, blueprints of various technologies, etc..
it's really about industry espionage, gathering intellectual property.
And this one was conducted by several groups, for example, the Winnti and the Apt41 group or a group called barium, and they all were participating as part of this operation and this big campaign as part of our case study.
