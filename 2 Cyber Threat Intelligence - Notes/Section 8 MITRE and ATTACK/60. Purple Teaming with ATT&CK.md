### Lecture Flow

Another very important topic when operationalizing [[2 Cyber Threat Intelligence - Notes/Section 7 CTI Related Frameworks/Diamond model, LM Cyber Kill Chain, MITRE ATTACK|attack]] is to do effective purple teaming with attack.
If you don't know what purple teaming is, just go back to the basics.
I explained [[2 Cyber Threat Intelligence - Notes/Section 2 Basics of SOC/10. Blue, Red and Purple Teaming|blue, red and purple teaming]] over there, but by now I assume that what it is.
let's have a look at how a purple team could leverage attack to do [[2 Cyber Threat Intelligence - Notes/Section 6 Cyber Threat Intelligence(CTI)/29. Intelligence, Threat Intelligence and Cyber Threat Intelligence|threat]] informed decision making.

### The first one is the purple team would identify ttps that are relevant t

The first one is the purple team would identify ttps that are relevant to the organization, and this would be again done in a collaborative effort, because that's what we want to achieve with the purple team.
You have the knowledge of the attackers.
You have the knowledge of the defenders.
If both of them come together, you have great synergies.

### And then in the purple team, the guys and girls that do the blue team st

And then in the purple team, the guys and girls that do the blue team stuff, they would build [[2 Cyber Threat Intelligence - Notes/Section 8 MITRE and ATTACK/50. Detections|detections]] for those ttps that are relevant and the guys and girls that do the red team stuff, they would build additional capabilities to execute those ttps, because that would allow you to simulate them and to test whether your detections are successful and would also make your detections better.
And then both groups come together as well, and they would simulate the ttps.
They would check whether detections are good and whether capabilities are good.
And in the end they would have lessons learned.

### They would see that certain detections don't perform as well as expected

They would see that certain detections don't perform as well as expected, for example, on the blue team.
But the red team would also learn that, for example, certain detections are quite good and in fact they need to ramp up their capabilities.
This would also, increase the overall security, but it would also maybe lead to an adaption in defenses because there may be some tweaks required.
And then from there we simply go back and we repeat this process because this is a never ending story.

### And then the purple team would again start to identify Ttps and the enti

And then the purple team would again start to identify Ttps and the entire process would continue.
Yeah, that's the entire idea of doing this on a continuous basis.
And that's how you can leverage attack to do a threat informed decision making with your purple, red and blue team.
